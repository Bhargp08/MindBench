# MindBench Participant Consent Form

**Status:** DEFERRED DRAFT. Not needed while Bharg is the only participant; to be finalized and approved before anyone else takes part. This is a personal, preregistered self-experiment run by Bharg Patel. No institutional review board review has been obtained, and nobody involved is claiming institutional oversight.

## Purpose
MindBench is a set of short computer tasks measuring attention, perception and memory. The main question is whether naturally varying sleep, or time of day, changes how people perform. Bharg is the main participant. A small number of adult friends may take part as pilot participants to help improve the instructions.

## What you will do
- Do a session of five short tasks in a web browser on a laptop or desktop with a keyboard: a contrast-detection task, an attention-cueing task, a color-word task, a working-memory task and a word-recall task.
- Before each session, answer a short questionnaire: hours slept last night, bed and wake times, a sleepiness rating, whether you had caffeine, and your device.
- Each session takes about **25–35 minutes. This is an estimate**; it will be timed during the pilot (MB-13) and updated here.
- Pilot participants do a few sessions. You are not asked to change your sleep, caffeine or routine in any way. **Sleep is only recorded, never restricted.**
- Optionally, you may be asked for short feedback (think-aloud notes or a short interview). No audio is recorded unless you separately agree to it here: [ ] I agree to audio recording of feedback sessions.

## Voluntary participation
Taking part is voluntary. There is no payment and no pressure, and saying no has no consequences. You may stop any session at any time, and skip any question.

## Withdrawal and deletion
You can withdraw at any time, and ask for your data to be deleted, by telling Bharg directly (contact: [Bharg to add a contact method]). Give your participant code so your data can be found. Data already released in a public dataset cannot be pulled back, which is why open release is a separate choice below and is not the default.

## What is stored
- A random participant code (no names, emails or IP addresses are stored in the dataset).
- Your trial-by-trial responses and reaction times, task scores and the task order.
- Questionnaire answers (sleep hours, bed and wake times, sleepiness, caffeine). **Sleep and sleepiness are health-related, so they are kept private.**
- Session information: date and time, time zone, browser, device and screen size.
- Any feedback notes, stored as anonymized notes.

## Who sees your data
Only Bharg sees the raw data. It is stored privately (see `data_management.md`). Summaries or aggregates may appear in a write-up or dashboard. Your data will not be published at trial level unless you tick the box below.
