# Self-Experiment Policy

Status: DRAFT for Bharg's approval.

This is a personal, preregistered self-experiment (N = 1). No institutional review board review has been obtained; never describe it as institutionally reviewed or overseen.

## No induced sleep loss
Sleep is observed, not manipulated: the design uses natural night-to-night variation, logged by questionnaire. Nobody is asked to shorten, delay or skip sleep, or change caffeine use. If anyone proposes deliberate sleep deprivation, stop and flag it.

## Stop rules
Stop a session, and do not make it up, if you feel unwell, dizzy or in pain, are too drowsy to continue safely, feel distressed, or simply don't want to continue. The session is recorded as incomplete and handled by the preregistered missing-data rules.

## Other participants
Not in scope now. Adding any (adults only, with written consent) requires the consent form first, and recruitment beyond friends needs proper institutional oversight.

The doc lint rejects wording that claims institutional approval. This project has none.
