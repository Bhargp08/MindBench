# Self-Pilot and Self-Experiment Policy

**Status:** DRAFT for Bharg's approval.

## Scope and honesty
This is a personal, preregistered self-experiment (N = 1 main participant) with a small, consented pilot of adult friends. No institutional review board review has been obtained. Never describe it as institutionally reviewed or overseen in the README, the paper, the preregistration or any application.

## No induced sleep loss
- Sleep is **observed, not manipulated**. The design uses natural night-to-night variation, logged by questionnaire.
- Nobody, Bharg included, is asked to shorten, delay or skip sleep, or to change caffeine use, for this study.
- If anyone proposes deliberate sleep deprivation, stop and flag it. It is out of scope.

## Stop rules
Stop a session, and do not make it up later, if:
- You feel unwell, dizzy, or have a headache or eye strain.
- You are drowsy enough that continuing would be unsafe (for example, you should not do sessions while driving or otherwise unsafe).
- You feel distressed or just don't want to continue. No reason is needed.
- Bharg may pause the study for a participant at any time.

If a session is stopped, it is recorded as incomplete and excluded under the preregistered missing-data rules.

## Participants
- Adults (18+) only, with a signed consent form (`consent_form.md`). No minors.
- No payment, no pressure. Withdrawal and deletion are always allowed.

## Rule note (for lint)
The repo's doc lint rejects wording that claims institutional approval of this project. That wording is only allowed in this folder when explaining the rule, and this project has no such approval.
